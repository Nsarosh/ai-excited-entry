# Recorded visual iterations

Six distinct composition passes were rendered and reduced to 300 x 300 for review during the recorded build. Preview files are under `previews/`.

1. **Pass 1 — initial focal placement:** portal at (512, 424), 250 px radius, muted ray weight. Checked thumbnail; the centered opening felt static and the figure did not anchor the scale.
2. **Pass 2 — scale and offset:** portal moved right to (565, 414), enlarged to 270 px, rays lengthened. Checked thumbnail; the off-center mass gave the eye a clearer path but needed a stronger bright center.
3. **Pass 3 — focal contrast:** boosted colored radial streaks and the white nucleus. Checked thumbnail; focal hierarchy improved, while the text still needed a reliable dark backing.
4. **Pass 4 — stronger threshold and rings:** enlarged the aperture to 286 px and strengthened the broken rings, preserving the open upper-right rim. Checked thumbnail; the effect read clearly, but peripheral detail was too loud.
5. **Pass 5 — final balance:** changed the portal interior from flat pink to a hot orange-to-magenta rim gradient, enlarged the white-hot core, and strengthened the figure's cyan edge and silhouette. Kept the rays restrained and the hand-built caption plaque compact. Re-checked the 300 px thumbnail; the scale twist and figure remain legible without losing the single focal point.
6. **Pass 6 — unmistakable breach:** kept pass 5's off-center portal, rays, palette and hand-built caption, then cut one crisp jagged dark slit through the white-hot core with narrow cyan and gold lips. Slightly broadened and lengthened the cliff figure for thumbnail legibility. Reviewed both 1024 px and 300 px renders; the doorway reads as a split in the core while the luminous center remains visible.

The actual rendered PNG previews document each pass; this log records their compositional intent.
